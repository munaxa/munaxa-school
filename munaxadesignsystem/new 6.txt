# Mission

You are a Principal Design Systems Engineer and Staff Product Designer.

Review the entire repository and transform it into a production-grade design system for Munaxa School OS.

Do not make cosmetic-only changes.

Munaxa is a School Operating System.

* ERP
* SIS
* School Website

Munaxa manages:

* Students
* Attendance
* Teachers
* Admissions
* Finance
* Communication
* Transport
* HR
* Reports
* School Operations

The design system will become the single source of truth for:

* Admin Portal
* Parent Portal
* Teacher Portal
* Mobile App
* Marketing Site
* Future Products
