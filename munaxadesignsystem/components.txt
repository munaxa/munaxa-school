Munaxa is a School Operating System.

Core domains:

- Students
- Parents
- Teachers
- Attendance
- Admissions
- Finance
- Transport
- Communication
- Reports
- Settings

The design system must contain reusable business components for these domains.

The goal is to make AI agents capable of generating complete Munaxa modules while maintaining consistency.
