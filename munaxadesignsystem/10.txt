# Mission

You are a Principal Product Architect, Enterprise UX Architect, and Design Systems Lead.

Review the entire Munaxa Design System.

The system already contains:

- Design Tokens
- Components
- School Components
- Patterns
- Templates
- Accessibility
- RTL Support
- Dark Mode
- Notifications
- Permissions
- Workflows

DO NOT redesign the visual identity.

DO NOT change colors.

DO NOT change typography.

DO NOT change spacing philosophy.

DO NOT replace existing components.

Your goal is to elevate Munaxa from a Design System into a Product Operating System comparable to Salesforce, Workday, and ServiceNow.

Focus on information architecture, record-centric design, workspace design, and enterprise workflows.

Everything in Munaxa must revolve around Records.

Examples:

Student
Teacher
Parent
Applicant
Invoice
Payment
